## 1. Who we are

Posted Automator (“we”, “the Service”) is a private automation tool operated by [YOUR_NAME / YOUR_COMPANY] for publishing content to the operator’s own TikTok account.

## 2. What data we collect

We may collect and process:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| TikTok OAuth tokens (access, refresh) | Publish content on your behalf after you authorize |
| TikTok user identifier (open_id) | Link posts to your account |
| Google Drive file metadata and content | Download videos and captions you place in your folder |
| Proxy connection details | Route API requests per your configuration |
| Technical logs (errors, timestamps) | Operate and secure the Service |

We do **not** collect your TikTok password.

## 3. How we use data

- To connect your TikTok account via OAuth  
- To read media you store in your designated Google Drive folder  
- To upload and publish content only when you have authorized the Service  
- To maintain security, debugging, and reliability  

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 4. Where data is stored

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the operator (e.g. local server or VPS), including MongoDB and local file storage. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest.

## 5. Third parties

We interact with:

- **TikTok** — OAuth and Content Posting API ([TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy))  
- **Google** — Google Drive API ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))  

We only share data with these providers as required to operate the Service.

## 6. Retention

- OAuth tokens: until you disconnect or delete your account in the Service  
- Media files: until processing completes and cleanup runs  
- Logs: limited retention for operations (configurable)  

## 7. Your rights

You may:

- Revoke TikTok access in TikTok app settings  
- Request deletion of stored tokens and account data by contacting us  
- Stop using the Service at any time  

## 8. Security

We use encryption for sensitive credentials, environment-based secrets, and access controls on the admin panel. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## 9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13.
